It's only you... and the Citadel


This website is dedicated to Citadel, also known as Cytadela in Poland, the 3D first-person shooter game developed by Virtual Design for the Commodore Amiga and released in 1995. Beyond the game itself, it explores the broader fictional universe in which Citadel is set, including its lore, stories, reinterpretations, and creative offshoots. The site is a fan-driven project maintained by some members of the original development team. If you want to get in touch or contribute, please reach out on Discord or Reddit.

We hope you enjoy your visit and rediscover the world of Citadel!
